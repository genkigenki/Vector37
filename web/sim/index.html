Laid out like the SMK-37 Pro: wheels, the screen with its six buttons, K1..K8 over the 16 pads, the faders over the 2 x 6 buttons, the keys below. The pads are four groups of 2 x 2: in each group the four pads are the four corners, as on the screen (SELECT, MODE, FILTER, MUTE). No planet selected (a SELECT pad tapped again): the knobs are the orbit. A held button lends the knobs another set (hold it with the mouse, turn a knob with the scroll wheel). Computer keyboard: 1–8 = top pads, A W S E D F T G Y H U J K = keys, Z / X = octave. A MIDI keyboard plays the keys. The control map: docs/CONTROLS.md.
